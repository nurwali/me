How I work

From brief to final cut.

I usually develop the concept and storyboards myself. Clients sign off on the story before a single frame moves.

  1. 01

    Script & concept

    Find the one idea viewers should remember about the product.

  2. 02

    Storyboard

    A frame-by-frame plan the client approves before production.

  3. 03

    Styleframes

    Lock the look: type, color, product UI and illustration style.

  4. 04

    Animatic

    Timing and voiceover in a rough cut, so pacing is fixed early.

  5. 05

    Animation & edit

    Motion, sound and edit, with AI where it adds speed or scale.

  6. 06

    Review & delivery

    Quality review, feedback rounds and every format the client needs.
