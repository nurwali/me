NUR MOHAMMAD KHAN — STATIC HTML WEBSITE

OPTION 1: ONE FILE
Upload the self-contained index.html from the single-file download to your web root.
It includes Home, Portfolio and Pricing through #home, #work and #pricing links.
Images, font, styles and scripts are embedded. No WordPress, PHP, plugins or build step.
To edit settings, search for PORTFOLIO_CONFIG inside index.html.

OPTION 2: THREE PAGES
Unzip Nur-Khan-HTML-Website.zip. Upload index.html, portfolio.html, pricing.html and the entire assets folder to your hosting web root (often public_html).
Keep the folder structure intact. No setup or build command is required.

EDITING
Edit ordinary text in the HTML files.
Edit assets/config.js for your email, phone, booking URL, four service prices, and approved client reviews.
Replace assets/nur-khan-portrait.png to update the portrait; retain transparency.
The bottom portrait fade is controlled by --gd-portrait-fade in assets/styles.css.
Change data-video on project buttons to replace a Vimeo video; replace its poster image too.
The nine supplied Vimeo videos remain included. Founder Led Motion has no supplied footage and displays an honest empty state.

CONTENT DEFAULTS
Prices say “Let’s discuss” until you enter your rates.
Testimonials remain hidden until approved reviews are supplied in the config.
Without a scheduling URL, visitors can email you to arrange a call. A Calendly URL activates the calendar; another HTTPS scheduling URL creates a booking button.
Vimeo playback needs internet access and permission to embed the videos. No live booking was made in testing.
